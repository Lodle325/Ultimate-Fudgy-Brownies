      Frequently Asked Questions

      Why did my brownies sink in the middle?

      Usually over‑mixing or under‑baking. Mix until just combined, and trust the toothpick test.

      Can I use cocoa powder only?

      Yes, but you’ll miss the fudgy texture. Use ¾ cup cocoa + ¼ cup melted butter as a substitute.

      How do I get that crinkly top?

      Dissolve sugar completely in warm butter, and beat eggs really well – that’s the secret!
